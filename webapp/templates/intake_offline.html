How It Works

Offline workflow

1. Capture everything on one device Fill the intake form, add photos, and attach bookkeeping images even when there is no reliable signal.
2. Queue the submission locally Kumbu stores the full payload in local offline storage so the browser can be closed and reopened later.
3. Reconnect and let sync finish Once online, the app pushes the intake answers and files into the same Kumbu provisioning flow used by the Google Form sync.
